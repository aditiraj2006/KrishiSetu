# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free environment for all contributors, regardless of experience level, identity, or background.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment:

- Be respectful and kind to all community members
- Welcome and support newcomers in the community
- Provide constructive feedback
- Accept criticism gracefully
- Focus on what is best for the community
- Show empathy toward other contributors

## Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, discrimination, or abusive language
- Offensive comments or personal attacks
- Publishing private information without consent
- Trolling or disruptive behavior
- Any form of discrimination (sexism, racism, etc.)
- Intimidation or exclusionary conduct

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing acceptable behavior standards.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces, GitHub discussions, issues, pull requests, and community interactions.

## Reporting Violations

If you witness or experience violations of this Code of Conduct, report confidentially to:

📧 Email: aditiraj0205@gmail.com  
📊 GitHub: Create an issue tagged with `[Code of Conduct]`

All reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially.

## Enforcement

Contributors violating the Code of Conduct may receive:

- Warning
- Temporary restriction
- Permanent removal from the community
